**Marion County School Board, District 5**

Sarah James is seeking re-election to the Marion County School Board District 5 seat. Her challengers are Donald “Donny” Barber and Ualthan Bigby. The race is nonpartisan meaning all voters in the District will vote on these candidates in the primary election on August 18.

Sarah James was first elected in 2022 with approximately 65% of the vote and has served on the Board since November of that year. She currently serves as Chair of the Marion County School Board.
