Candidate Profiles by Jennifer Solida - Ocala/Marion Citizens for Sustainable Development, Follow on Facebook at https://www.facebook.com/groups/505707795175307/about

Marion County School Board District 4 is an open seat this year. Current School Board member Nancy Thrower is not seeking re-election.

Four candidates are running:

• Jeff Brewer

• Mike Mills

• Brigitte Smith

• Earl “Buddy” Wyckoff

This is a nonpartisan School Board race. If no candidate receives more than 50% of the vote in August, the top two candidates advance to November.
