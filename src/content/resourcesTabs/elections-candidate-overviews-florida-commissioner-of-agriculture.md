**Commissioner of Agriculture - State of Florida**

In the Primary, two Democrats are facing off and two Republicans are facing off. The winner of each face off in November at the midterm. The incumbent Florida Commissioner of Agriculture & Consumer Services is Wilton Simpson, Republican. He is being challenged by Matt “The Welder” Taylor. The Democrats are Joey Atkins and Donald Pritchard.

Candidate for Florida Commissioner of Agriculture & Consumer Services

Florida’s Commissioner of Agriculture leads the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, or FDACS.

That means this office has responsibilities involving agriculture, food safety, consumer protection, forestry, agricultural environmental services and programs designed to help protect working farms and ranches.

For those of us concerned about Florida losing agricultural land to development, one of the most important tools connected to this office is the Rural and Family Lands Protection Program, which helps preserve privately owned working farms and ranches through voluntary conservation easements.

So when we look at these candidates, we believe voters should be asking not only whether they “support agriculture,” but also: How will they help keep farming economically viable? How will they protect working land? And how will they balance preservation with private property rights?
