**MARION COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS - DISTRICT 2**

Four Republican candidates will face off in the primary for District 2. The winner will face a Write-In candidate in November meaning the primary is closed, only Republicans can vote in this race. The candidates are:

* Matt Cretul
* Mike Crimi
* Erica Lynn Hay
* Brien Weidemiller
