  right to free and fair elections.

1.  **Check your registration and keep checking it regularly.** Every state has an online system to check your registration, or visit [Vote.org](http://vote.org). Voter purge efforts are being pursued by Trump and his enablers, and it’s possible that this fall the Supreme Court will change the law to allow states to kick people off the voting rolls just days before the election. The best way to prevent this is to make sure your registration is up to date, check it regularly through the election, and help others do the same. 
2. **Get all your ID in order and encourage others to do the same**. This shouldn’t be necessary; however, we know that in many states there are, or will be, new requirements. **We need to ensure everyone is planning ahead**. **Now** is the time to make sure you have a current ID that meets your state’s requirements, *and to* find or apply for a certified copy of your birth certificate or passport, if you can. If the SAVE Act becomes law, it will be devastating for millions, especially anyone who has changed their name for marriage or other reasons. The more you prepare yourself for whatever may happen, the more time you’ll have to help others ensure they can vote. 
3. **Consider becoming a poll worker** in your community by applying with your Supervisor of Elections office to be a poll worker. Poll workers are hired on as needed basis, are required to complete training and to work long hours during elections. Positions are not always available.
4. **Or, consider becoming a poll watcher** on the days of early voting, on primary election day, or election day. Poll watchers are trained volunteers of the political parties with candidates on the ballot or of the candidates. They receive training in spotting irregularities or voting challenges, for instance long lines at polls, and are given tools to report those issues. To volunteer, contact the office of the political party of your choice in your community (for instance, the Marion County Democratic Party).
5. **Volunteer** for meaningful actions you can take at home with organizations working to protect Voter Rights nationally 

***VoteRiders is** a nonpartisan, non-profit organization that helps eligible U.S. citizens obtain the proper identification required to vote. They help eliminate barriers to voting by covering associated document fees (such as birth certificates) and providing free transportation to ID-issuing offices.*
