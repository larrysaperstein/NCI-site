**WHAT DOES A CIRCUIT JUDGE ACTUALLY DO?**

Florida circuit judges handle some of the most serious and complicated cases that come through our court system. Depending on their assignment, that can include felony criminal cases, major civil disputes, family law, juvenile matters, probate, guardianship and mental-health cases.

Circuit judges serve six-year terms.

So when choosing a judge, voters aren’t simply choosing an attorney with an impressive resume. We’re choosing someone who may be responsible for interpreting and applying the law, protecting due process, managing complicated cases and making decisions that can profoundly affect people’s lives.
