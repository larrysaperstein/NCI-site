[](https://5calls.org/)5 Calls, [https://5calls.org/,](https://5calls.org/) makes it easy for you to reach your members of Congress and make your voice heard. They research issues, write clearly articulated scripts, figure out the most influential decision-makers, and collect phone numbers for their offices. 

All you have to do is call.

You can select targeted calls for your community, state, or at the national level.
