FLIC (Florida Immigrant Coalition) is a statewide coalition of 83 member organizations and over 100 allies, founded in 1998 and formally incorporated in 2004. They are led by their membership, including grassroots and community organizations, farmworkers, youth, advocates, lawyers, union members, and more.  FLIC's main office is in Miami-Dade, but they have team members in Broward, Palm Beach, Orange, Osceola and Duval Counties. They offer pro-immigrant advocacy, education and community building across the state including naturalization and legal support clinics, our community events, civic engagement activities and hosting the RAISE Hotline, which provides confidential support to communities and individuals supporting immigrant enforcement actions.
