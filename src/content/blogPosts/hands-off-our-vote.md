National Training Call was July 30. Thousands around the country joined. Threats are real but our election systems are also strong. There are many scenarios threatening elections some which have already happened including gerrymandering, changing ID requirements, and some which are looming. There are small actions everyone can take now. Nature Coast Indivisible will be involved in several ways. Supporters are already signing up to be poll watchers, some have become poll workers. We will work the phones and canvass to encourage voters to vote early and take advantage of Vote By Mail and assist with ballot curing efforts and more.
