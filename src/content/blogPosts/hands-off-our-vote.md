On July 30, Indivisible hosted the first of several national training calls focused on election protection. Thousands of people around the country joined, including members of the NCI leadership team. The presenters pointed out that the threats to elections by Trump and his enablers are real but our election systems are strong and we must start now to protect and support them. They emphasized that there are many scenarios that threaten elections some which have already occurred including gerrymandering in several states and changing ID requirements in others, more threats are looming. There are small actions everyone can take now (see Resources Page on Election Protection). Nature Coast Indivisible will be involved in several ways. Supporters are signing up to be poll watchers, some have become poll workers, and others are training to to help "cure ballots." We will work the phones and canvass to encourage Vote-By-Mail and Early Voting, and we are ready to be messengers of accurate information about election procedures and election threats.
