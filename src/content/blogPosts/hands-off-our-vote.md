On July 30, Indivisible hosted the first of several national training call focused on election protection. Thousands of people around the country joined including members of the NCI leadership team. The presenters pointed out that the threats to elections by Trump and his enablers are real but our election systems are also strong and we must start now to protect and support those systems. They emphasized that there are many scenarios threatening elections some which have already happened including gerrymandering, changing ID requirements, and more are looming. There are small actions everyone can take now and those are now included on our Resources Page on Election Protection. Nature Coast Indivisible will continue to be involved in several ways. Supporters are already signing up to be poll watchers, some have become poll workers and others are training to to help "cure ballots." We will work the phones and canvass to encourage voters to vote early and take advantage of Vote By Mail and we are ready to be messengers of accurate information about election procedures and threats that might arise in our communities.
