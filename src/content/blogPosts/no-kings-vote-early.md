***On October 17, NCI joined thousands around the country by hosting a No Kings Early Vote event. Since our early vote window was scheduled to start several days later and last a week we picked an event to create visual reminders to our local community. We sent the following to our supporters in advance:***

***Why is NCI making hats and decorating cars and banners instead of protesting on October 17?***

Scholars say we are at an inflection point in stopping the rise of authoritarianism. Trump, his enablers, and all authoritarians are afraid of THE VOTE and of voters. We must act on what we know they fear, it is an important strategy for success. They demonize vote-by-mail and early voting, and have gerrymandered districts. The antidote is for Americans to cast their votes.

*No Kings, No Thrones, We Decide!*

***What if we encourage voting by people who vote against what we support? What about election protection? How do we stop election sabotage?***

Building a constituency of voters is critical to keeping voting alive in the future. Critical to election protection is ensuring an election too big to rig, too big to ignore.  Voting Early is essential to guaranteeing massive voter turnout. Across the country, the best candidates will win important races. 
*Vote Now or Bow Down Later!*

***What can I do?***

This brings us to hats, cars, and banners, visual reminders of Voting Early.

You can join NCI to create and wear hats, decorate and drive cars, make and share buttons, and create messages on Big A\*\* Banners that promote Voting Early.

(*Political scholar Heather Cox Richardson discussed the importance of visual reminders during her Oct. 4th, "WTH Just Happened" talk with Joanne Freeman!*)

**Saturday, October 17, 10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.**

Tunes, snacks, and more!

Unitarian Universalist Fellowship, 7533 N. Florida Ave., Citrus Springs
