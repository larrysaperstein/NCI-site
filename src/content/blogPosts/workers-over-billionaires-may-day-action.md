### **People Over Profits: Nature Coast Indivisible Activists Take to OverPass for 12 Hours with Message in Solidarity with National May Day Actions** 

**\[Ocala, Florida]** — On Friday, May 1, activists with Nature Coast Indivisible (NCI) will hoist a billboard-sized banner reading “People Over Profits” for 12-hours, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., over I-75 on the SW 43rd Street overpass in Ocala. They will join actions taking place in over 1,000 cities nationwide, organized by May Day Strong to demand,“Workers Over Billionaires.”

Nancy Donaldson, spokesperson for NCI, pointed out that nearly 1 in 4 children in Marion County is living in poverty. Although nearly 60,000 people are living in poverty in our community alone, Florida legislators would rather spend their time changing voting rules fighting for solutions to the affordability crisis.” 

Donaldson also noted that teachers in Florida and in Marion County are among the lowest-paid in the country. “We ask teachers to take on enormous responsibilities caring for and teaching our children, but they are paid wages so low that many hold a second job just to get by. This is happening while billionaires are getting more tax breaks and Americans find themselves paying for a war costing billions of dollars each day”. 

“Thousands of drivers pass under the SW 43rd Street overpass every day. The message “People over Profits” brings attention to the need for change in the current political landscape, where the rich curry favors to their own benefit and ordinary Americans lose out”.

Nature Coast Indivisible is committed to supporting principles of democracy, equity, and justice. It is a local chapter of Indivisible, [indivisible.org](http://indivisible.org).

Thousands of businesses and workers have signed on to the “No Work, No School, No Shopping” pledge to demand billionaires pay their fair share, to demand ICE out of communities, and to demand the protection and expansion of democracy for working people. For more on national May Day actions, visit [MayDayStrong.org](http://maydaystrong.org).
