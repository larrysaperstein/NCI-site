Nature Coast Indivisible members joined Citrus Indivisible on Saturday, July 25 to protest deadly immigration enforcement and demand systemic policy changes to ICE operations. Lorenzo Salgado Araugo was a 52-year-old Mexican father of three who was shot and killed by ICE agents on July 7, 2026, in Houston, Texas, while heading to work. Johan Sebastian Guerreo was a 26-year-old Colombian father of a young toddler who was shot and killed by ICE agents on July 13, 2026, in Biddeford, Maine. Their killings followed the ICE killings of Renee Good and Alex Pretti. ICE operatives have also been responsible for untold numbers of acts of violence in our communities and in detention centers which are often being run by for-profit corporations.
