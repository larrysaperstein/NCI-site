Primary Election Day is Tuesday, August 18. Marion County candidates are vying in nonpartisan races for two school board seats and one judge seat that will be decided by all voters based on the results of the primary. Republican candidates are competing in two county-wide races. The winner in those primaries will face a write-in candidate in November resulting in the primaries being closed, only registered Republicans can vote in these races. See our Resources Pages for detailed overviews of candidates. Several state-wide primary races are taking place on August 18, voters will decide who will represent Democrats and Republicans for Governor, Commissioner of Agriculture,  and U.S. Senate and House seats as well as seats of State Senators and Representatives.
