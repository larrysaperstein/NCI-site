No Kings: Vote Early is a nationwide day of action to turn people power into voter power. The truth is: people don't decide whether to vote in a vacuum. Experience tells us that the more people who vote early, the less opportunity there is for overt sabotage or accidental events that keep people from the polls. Vote Early is a form of election protection. That's why October 17 is designed to make early voting visible, social, and impossible to miss. Across the country, there will be rallies, block parties, cookouts, and walks to the polls where book clubs, pickleball groups, knitting circles, or paddling clubs go vote together. Please feel free to bring along your own shirts, hats, and snacks to share but give us a heads up for our planning by email naturecoastindivisible@gmail.com!
