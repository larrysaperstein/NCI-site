subheading: Mail Your Ballot or Vote Early
---
Voting by mail is safe and secure. Deadlines to receive a vote by mail ballot differ by county.  Once received, complete your ballot ASAP, sign the back of the envelope and return it. You may mail your ballot or deposit it a secure ballot intake station (dropbox). You an also sign up for ballot tracking so you will know when your ballot has been received and has been counted. If you prefer to vote in person, go early to insure long lines or last minute events do not interfere with your chance to cast your ballot. Early voting days, hours, and locations can be found at the website of the Supervisor of Elections office for your county or by phoning them with your questions. See the Resources pages for more details.
