Defeating authoritarianism and the MAGA agenda starts with having the right tools in our hands— and a great community by our side. Join us weekly on Wednesdays for our **Midday Mingle & Mobilize**, a lively, action-focused gathering where we will get our facts straight, sharpen our activist skills, and make a tangible impact together. **When:** Wednesdays at 1:00 p.m. (6 weeks, September 9, 16*, 23, 30)
**Where:** Dunnellon Library, address, *Location for Sept. 16 TBAEach mingle will include 1) T**he Latest Scoop:** discuss emerging political stories of the week so we have our facts straight 2) The How-To-Hour: Learn skills like how to register new voters, use simple digital tools like Sosha, make stress-free voter calls, knock doors for democracy, cure ballots, etc. and 3) The Ten Minute Mobilizer: collectively complete a manageable tangible task.Bring your energy, your questions, and your creativity! RSVP is optional; however, it does help with planning to know how many people to expect. Email: naturecoastindivisible@gmail.com.See you there!!
